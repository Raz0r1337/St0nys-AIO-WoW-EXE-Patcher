No. 69 more lights: ground gets lights 4-8 too (c236-c250) [BETA]

The terrain shader (Terrain.bls, variants 64-127) only gets the 3 nearest
point lights per chunk in its own registers c28-c36 (0x7D0050), so with
more lights the ground still showed only 3 of them, with seams at chunk
edges.

- Hook at VA 0x7D0292 (mov ecx,[0xC5DF88] right before the upload of
  c0-c36): lights 4-8 of the chunk's local light list [ebp-0x104] - the 4th
  inline light and the side list of this patch - go to VS c236-c250 in the
  terrain format: position world minus camera ([0xCD8F5C], w = 1), colour
  (w = 0), attenuation (w = 0); free slots all 0 with attenuation x = 1,
  as the original does for c28-c36. Only if the card has at least 251 VS
  registers.
- The local list is cleared per chunk (0x790440 -> 0x834900 memsets 0xD4
  bytes), so its side list starts fresh for every chunk.
- Built into this patch, not selectable on its own: models and ground both
  write from c236 on and each shader reads right after its own upload.
  Shader selection is unchanged.
- Section .lght grows from 0x590 to 0x7B4 bytes; byte table rebuilt.
- PATCHES DE/EN: ground part and its register layout.
