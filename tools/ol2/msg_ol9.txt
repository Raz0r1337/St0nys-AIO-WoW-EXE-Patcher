No. 68 outline: glowing outline like in retail, thin line for the raid [BETA]

The outline is now bright right at the figure and fades out over about
6 pixels, in three steps of 2 pixels: inside the color halfway towards
white, in the middle about 60 %, outside about 25 % opaque.

- Each step gets eight shifted copies (2, 4 and 6 pixels); only the
  inner step stores the figure's depth, so things drawn later behind the
  semi-transparent steps (e.g. water) stay visible.
- Stencil: figure 0xC0, outline step | category (inside 0x80, middle
  0x40, outside 0). Higher values win, so the inner step always beats
  the outer ones; later drawn things compare the category bits only
  (mask 0x3F).
- Raid members outside your own party of 5 only get the inner step in
  the full color, as before - this keeps the cost of the raid outline
  down. You, your party, target and mouseover glow.
- Section .outl grows from 0x14F0 to 0x16AC bytes.
- PATCHES DE/EN: description, FPS note and how it works.
