No. 68 outline: draw outlined figures after all other opaque models [BETA]

"Only where hidden" did not see objects that are drawn after the
figure, e.g. a lantern in Dalaran, a mailbox or a plate in front of the
character. The game sorts the opaque M2 parts by model, shader and
texture instead of by distance, and draws batched doodads
(M2BatchDoodads, on by default) after them.

- Sorting pass 0 of the world scene (VA 0x822FDD): parts of outlined
  units get type 0x10 for the sort and so end up last; among themselves
  they keep the game's order (0x81EAD0). Afterwards they get their type
  back.
- Drawing pass 0 (VA 0x823D1F/0x823D3B): these parts are held back and
  drawn after the batched doodads.
- So everything else is already in the depth buffer when the outlined
  figures are drawn.
- Section .outl grows from 0x1310 to 0x14F0 bytes; byte table: the three
  new hook sites.
- PATCHES DE/EN: note on "only where hidden" and how it works.
