No. 68 outline: soft glow from a real blur instead of three steps [BETA]

The three steps of 2 pixels are gone again: every outlined figure is back
to the thin 2-pixel line with eight copies. The glow now comes from a
real blur, for you, your party, target and mouseover.

- Before the thin lines, their outline pixels are colored in full
  opacity into a render target of their own (size and anti-aliasing like
  the current render target, same depth/stencil buffer), resolved with
  StretchRect and blurred by a ps_2_0 shader (Gaussian, 5 bilinear taps):
  down to half resolution, horizontally, vertically. Then it is laid over
  the image (premultiplied alpha, x4, saturated, RGB only) only where
  there is no outlined figure (stencil below 0x80).
- On top the thin line, lightened for figures with glow (5/8 of the
  color plus 0x5E per channel). Raid members outside your party of 5 keep
  the plain thin line without glow, unless they are target or mouseover.
- Render target, depth buffer and viewport are restored; viewport and
  scissor are also marked as changed for the game. The texture transform
  flags of stages 0-4 go back to the values in the game's state cache.
- Names above heads no longer disappear while something is outlined
  (e.g. while hovering an NPC): depth-writing things drawn after the
  outlined figures went through a stencil test LESS against WPROT, which
  discarded every pixel outside an outline. Now the stencil gets a
  "visible" bit (figure 0xC0, ring 0x40 | category); later depth-writing
  draws use ALWAYS, PASS ZERO with write mask 0xC0, so they are always
  drawn and only hide the outline where they lie in front. Post colors
  protected party/raid rings with mask 0x3F, so "through walls party/raid
  only" keeps them. The DrawPrimitive branch (VA 0x6A3706) now goes
  through the same check as DrawIndexedPrimitive.
- The game's state cache: only the entries post and glow change are
  invalidated now, not all of them - 0xFFFFFFFF is a real value for
  D3DRS_AMBIENT, so the game could skip setting it after the outline.
- The textures live in video memory and are released before
  IDirect3DDevice9::Reset and when the device is destroyed: the three
  calls of 0x690150 (format change, lost device, destroy) go through the
  patch first. Byte table: 4 new entries (incl. the DrawPrimitive site).
- If anything fails (e.g. not enough video memory), that device keeps
  the thin lines.
- Section .outl grows from 0x16AC to 0x26A7 bytes.
- PATCHES DE/EN: description, FPS note, how it works, pixel shader 2.0.
