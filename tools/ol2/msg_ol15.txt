No. 68 outline: companions, party in other buildings, calmer glow [BETA]

- Companions of outlined party/raid members (pets, companions, totems and
  other summoned units: CHARMEDBY/SUMMONEDBY/CREATEDBY of a non-player
  unit matches a group entry) are found by walking the game's object list
  (0x4D3730) and outlined in their owner's color. They count as party
  (through walls / only where hidden like the party), up to entry 45 so
  you, target and mouseover still fit.
- In pass 0 their parts are sorted after all other outlined parts (type
  0x11 instead of 0x10), so a companion in front of you or the party no
  longer triggers "only where hidden". Your own companions (and with
  "only where hidden" those of party/raid not in the list) are added
  without an outline, only for this order.
- Party figures the game culls before drawing (another building or room)
  are still added to the list of visible objects when they are in view,
  like the visible branch of 0x793060 does (new hook at 0x7934C6 in
  0x793450). With "through walls: all" this applies to every outlined
  figure. Byte table: 1 new entry.
- Raid members outside your party of 5 now get the same glow as everyone
  else; the thin line without glow is gone.
- Glow twitched on small movements: the binary ring jumps by whole
  pixels and the composite multiplied 8-bit steps by 4. Now the
  downsample uses a 4x4 tent (4 bilinear taps at +-0.75 texels) and the
  vertical blur pass applies the curve 1-(1-y)^2, y = sat(2.5*x), so the
  texture holds the final opacity at full 8 bits; composite gain 1. Two
  new ps_2_0 shaders, same passes and textures.
- Section .outl grows from 0x26A7 to 0x2C86 bytes.
- PATCHES DE/EN: companions, figures in other buildings, glow for the
  whole raid, glow filter.
