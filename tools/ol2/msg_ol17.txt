No. 68 outline: no line where a figure only sticks into the ground or a wall [BETA]

"Only where hidden" marked the outline wherever something lay a little in
front of the copies, including the ground in front of feet that sink
slightly into it and a wall a figure stands half inside. At a flat view the
ground in front of a sunk foot lies s / sin(angle) in front of it, more than
the old offset of 0.001 * distance^2 yards.

- All copies of an outlined part now lie at one depth: the front of the
  bounding sphere, c4 = (ZC + KZ) * c5, so z/w = ZC + KZ for every vertex.
  The outline only shows where something lies in front of the whole unit.
- The sphere comes from the M2 header (centre of the bounding box
  [[model+0x2C]+0x150]+0xA0/+0xAC, radius +0xB8) and the model->view
  matrix [model+0xF4] (VS c2-c5 are the projection only, 0x872C10).
- The front-most sphere of the drawn part (stored in batch) and its
  parents [model+0x48] is used, as far as they are the unit's model or
  mount (weapon -> figure -> mount; the rider hangs on the mount,
  0x73D793). Only the drawn model and its parent chain are read; the
  pointers in the table are only compared.
- Only models updated this frame ([model+0x3C] = [[model+0x28]+0x14]),
  loaded, with a header and radius > 0; otherwise the old c4 + KZ * c5.
  Camera inside the sphere: no outline.
- Section .outl grows from 0x2FE2 to 0x336E bytes.
- PATCHES DE/EN: only what lies in front of the whole figure counts.
