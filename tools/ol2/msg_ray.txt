No. 68 outline: collision rays decide whether a figure is hidden [BETA]

With "only where hidden" only what lies in front of the box around the
model counted, so a wall right in front of the figure gave no outline.
Depth alone cannot tell such a wall from the ground in front of a sunken
foot; the game's collision query can.

- Hook at VA 0x4FB036 (call 0x7E6480 between OnWorldUpdate with the camera
  update and RenderWorld, once per frame in the main thread): the original
  call, then the rays. Only in the world ([0xBD0792]), not while loading
  ([0xBD0790]), with a camera [[0xB7436C]+0x7E20] at a finite position.
- For each figure whose copies were drawn "only where hidden" in the last
  frame: 3 rays with TraceLine (0x7A3B70, flags 0x100111: terrain, WMO and
  M2 collision, GameObjects) from the camera to knee, chest and head (0.3,
  0.6, 0.9 of the height [obj+0x854]; mounted 0.55, 0.75, 0.95), the foot
  point from [vtbl+0x2C], the object fresh by GUID (0x4D4DB0) every time.
  Each ray stops 0.4 yards before its target; no ray under 1 or over 150
  yards. At most 18 rays per frame, in turn.
- A hit marks the figure as hidden at once and stores the points 0.1 yards
  behind the hits (world); it counts as free again only after 3 free checks
  in a row. Until then a free check drops stored points that lie further
  from the camera than the figure (it stepped out in front of the wall).
  Unknown GUID: free.
- Drawing: for a hidden figure the rearmost stored point, converted via the
  world matrix [model+0xB4] and [model+0xF4] of the first figure or mount
  in the chain, replaces the depth of the copies if it lies further back
  than the front of the box. Without rays in this frame nothing changes.
- Section .outl grows from 0x345B to 0x4559 bytes; byte table rebuilt.
- PATCHES DE/EN: collision rays.
