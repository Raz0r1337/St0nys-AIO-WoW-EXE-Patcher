Add the LuxShoulderCam loader (No. 31) from st0ny-dev

The loader for LuxShoulderCam.dll (shoulder camera by Stormhand) comes
over from st0ny-dev as No. 31, right after the Lexara loader. It is
still untested and rated [untested - DLL risky]: the loaded DLL may be
detected or blocked on public servers. It is not in any preset.

- Same patch bytes as in st0ny-dev: the one-time jump at VA 0x76E495
  leads through three free 16-byte gaps that call
  LoadLibraryA("LuxShoulderCam.dll"); file size and PE header stay
  unchanged.
- wow_optimize and Lexara loaders: notes on the Lux loader (Lexara loads
  LuxShoulderCam.dll by itself, so only one of the two is needed).
- README: thanks to Stormhand for the permission to include
  LuxShoulderCam.
- Patches from No. 31 on move by 1 (now 87 patches); byte table rebuilt.
