No. 68 outline: smaller margin before "hidden" counts - box front instead of sphere [BETA]

With "only where hidden" the copies lay on the front of the bounding
sphere. For a standing figure the radius comes mostly from its height, so
whatever hides it had to stand about 1 yard in front of the body; a table
right in front of the legs did not count.

- The copies now lie on the front of the M2 bounding box
  ([[model+0x2C]+0x150]+0xA0/+0xAC) plus 0.05 yards: the corner towards
  the camera, horizontally always; the bottom of the box only when the
  camera looks from below (what stands on the ground then lies in front),
  the full height for attachments such as weapons (a drawn model that is
  neither the unit's model nor its mount). Mounts and animal shapes keep
  hooves and paws behind that depth, which a simply smaller sphere would
  not.
- The distance part shrinks from 1 * KZ to 0.1 * KZ (0.1 * 0.001 *
  distance^2 yards).
- The radius in the M2 header is only checked now (finite and > 0); a NaN
  or infinite radius means no box, as before.
- Section .outl grows from 0x336E to 0x345B bytes.
- PATCHES DE/EN: the new margin and how the front is found.
