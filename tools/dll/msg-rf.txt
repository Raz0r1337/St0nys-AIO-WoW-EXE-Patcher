Remove the AwesomeWotlkLib loader from the Reforged preset

blocked on public servers. The Reforged preset is meant to be safe on
public servers as well, so the loader is no longer part of it. It stays
in the presets Billy's_Wow.exe and St0nys_Wow.exe.
