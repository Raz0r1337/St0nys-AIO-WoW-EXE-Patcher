Rate the AwesomeWotlkLib and WotLKExtensions loaders as [safe - DLL risky]

The loader patches themselves are safe, but the DLLs they load may be
detected or blocked on public servers. New yellow rating for this case.

- Patcher: new flag DllRisky on No. 28 and 29. The name shows
  [DLL risky], the start screen explains the tag, and before patching a
  yellow note lists the chosen patches: the patch itself is safe, only
  use the DLL on servers that allow it.
- Docs: status yellow [safe - DLL risky] in the overview and the PATCHES
  headings, a new row in the rating legend, a new item in the warnings
  list. The note in both PATCHES sections becomes a warning box.
- AwesomeWotlkLib stays on by default and in the Reforged preset.

Text only, no patch bytes change (byte table unchanged).
