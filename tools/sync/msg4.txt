Same naming for all DLL loaders

All four DLL loaders load their DLL with LoadLibraryA when WoW starts;
they only differ in where they hook in. The AwesomeWotlkLib and
WotLKExtensions loaders (No. 28 and 29) were still called
"Enable ... support", now they read "Load ... at startup" like the
wow_optimize and Lexara loaders.

Names and docs only, no patch bytes change (byte table unchanged).
