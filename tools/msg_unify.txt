Ratings: one bracket per patch, legend boxes removed

- README overview and PATCHES headings: several ratings share one
  bracket with a single dot in front, the stricter one wins (red before
  orange before yellow; green turns yellow when the exe grows), e.g.
  [safe - exe grows], [unsafe - untested ingame].
- README: the sentence under the legend explains the scheme with
  examples, including [untested - exe grows].
- PATCHES: the boxes that only repeated the legend text (unsafe /
  untested online / untested ingame) are removed; the "exe grows" notes
  keep only patch-specific details (section name and size, anti-cheat
  warnings). The intro points to the README for the meaning of the
  ratings.
- Patcher menu: warnings joined with " - " instead of ", ".
