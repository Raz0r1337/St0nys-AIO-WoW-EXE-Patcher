Remove No. 81 (CameraReforged) until its use is allowed

CameraReforged is a port of Zendevve's CameraReforged. Besides the
GPL-3.0, its repository carries a LICENSE.md with all rights reserved
that forbids porting its code into other projects. The permission we had
came from Stormhand and does not cover it. So the patch is taken out
until its author has agreed; it stays in the git history (last in
fe23f7b) and can come back from there.

- The patch entry and its helper Add-CameraReforged are removed.
- Patches 82-87 move to 81-86 (86 patches now).
- A Wow.exe patched with an older version that contains it is restored
  from patcher_state.ini as usual and patched again without it.
- Byte table rebuilt: only the CameraReforged entries are gone.
