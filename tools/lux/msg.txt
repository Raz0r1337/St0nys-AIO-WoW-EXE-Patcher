Add No. 31: load LuxShoulderCam.dll at startup [BETA]

LuxShoulderCam by Stormhand is an over-the-shoulder camera for 3.3.5a
(height and sideways offset, set in game with /lsc). So far only Lexara
loaded it: Lexara calls LoadLibraryA("LuxShoulderCam.dll") in its
DllMain right after its own start. The new loader does the same without
Lexara:

- The jump at VA 0x76E495 (to a plain ret, reached only once from the
  entry point, after the Lexara and wow_optimize loaders and before the
  voice.dll loader) now leads through three free 16-byte gaps
  (VA 0x6B68A0, 0x6B69C0, 0x6B6AE0). They put the name on the stack,
  call LoadLibraryA and return. File size and PE header stay unchanged.
- Only one of the two is needed: with Lexara (No. 30 or as dinput8.dll)
  this patch is not necessary. Both together do no harm, Windows loads a
  DLL with the same name only once per process.
- Not together with CameraReforged: both hook VA 0x6070CB, and Lux does
  not hook in there if CameraReforged is applied. Both descriptions say
  so.
- Untested, [BETA], deselected by default, not in any preset.

Patches from No. 31 on move up by one (89 patches now); all references
in the patcher and the docs are updated.

CameraReforged comes from Zendevve's CameraReforged, not from Stormhand.
Its author, source note and description are corrected, and the
acknowledgement for Stormhand now names LuxShoulderCam.

Byte table rebuilt: 4 new entries for No. 31, nothing else changed.
