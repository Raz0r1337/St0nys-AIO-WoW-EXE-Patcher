Add No. 68: outline for target and mouseover [BETA]

Brings the outline from st0ny-dev-outline into st0ny-dev for good; it no
longer needs a branch of its own. It was tested in game (with DXVK),
status "untested online", deselected by default and in no preset.

- The outline becomes No. 68 (after No. 67, pixel-exact icons); patches
  68-89 move to 69-90, references and the note lists in the README
  are moved along.
- The section helper Add-SectionBlock comes along again: when the PE
  header has no room for another section entry, the last appended
  section is extended instead. Add-CodeSection and Add-HdPortraits use
  it as in st0ny-dev-outline. With all seven patches that append a
  section, Visus (No. 84) now extends the section of No. 78.
- Code, entry and docs section are the ones from st0ny-dev-outline,
  only the number differs. A Wow.exe with only the outline, and one with
  the six section patches of st0ny-dev-outline, are byte-identical to
  the ones from st0ny-dev-outline.
- Byte table rebuilt.
