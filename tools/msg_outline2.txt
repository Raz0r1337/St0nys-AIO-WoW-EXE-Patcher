Outline: occluders drawn later hide the outline

Bushes, mushrooms and grass are drawn after the figures, so the outline
used to stay visible through them. Now:

- The figure itself is marked with stencil 4, the outline ring with 1/2.
- The eight shifted copies also write the figure's depth into the ring,
  so things behind the figure cannot clear it.
- Depth-writing draws after a figure was marked run in clear mode: where
  they pass the depth test they reset the stencil, so whatever lies in
  front of the figure hides its outline too. Transparent draws (no depth
  write) leave the outline in place.
- Further parts of the same figure (cloak, weapon, mount) are first drawn
  over their own ring without depth test, so no outline remains between
  the parts.

Section .outl grows to 0x9DA bytes; byte table rebuilt.
