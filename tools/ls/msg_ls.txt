No. 69 "Lights stay on when their source is off-screen" from st0ny-dev-lights [BETA]

The fix for vanishing point lights (No. 70 in st0ny-dev-lights) also helps
without new shaders, so dev gets it too: turning the camera so that a light
source (a torch, say) leaves the screen no longer switches its light off on
everything that is still visible.

- One byte at VA 0x81E548 (je -> jmp) in QueryLights 0x81E400: a light with
  an outdated time stamp stays active. Models still unregister their lights
  themselves when removed (0x824D30).

  Every patch from the old No. 69 on moves up by 1 (91 patches); references
  and the warning lists in the README follow.
